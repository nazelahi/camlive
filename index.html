A Complete Cue Sports Broadcast Suite

Everything you need to stream tournament matches with broadcast-quality graphics — no PC or OBS required.

Interactive Live Scoring

Snooker with frame tracking, live break calculator, foul points (4–7), and winner detection. Pool (8-Ball, 9-Ball, 10-Ball) with race-to targets, rack counters, and suit selection. Unlimited undo/redo keeps the score straight.

Multi-Protocol Streaming

RTMP & RTMPS for YouTube, Facebook, Twitch, Kick and Restream, plus SRT, RTSP and UDP. Hardware-accelerated H.264/H.265 encoding with adaptive bitrate and automatic background reconnection.

TV-Grade Scoreboard Themes

Neon, Heritage, Cyber Blade, Minimalist, Twin Pod and Corner Bug overlay themes. Add sponsor banners, pin them anywhere, and layer a permanent watermark badge over the feed.

Pro Camera Dock

Ergonomic horizontal dock with 10x digital zoom, exposure compensation (−2 to +2 EV), focus lock, HDR enhancement, torch, and eco screen-dim mode for low-light arenas and marathon matches.

Audio Studio Engine

Mic gain boost with real-time VU monitoring, dynamic range compressor/limiter to stop clipping on loud cue strikes, and support for Bluetooth lavalier and USB audio interfaces.

Wireless Remote Control

Built-in HTTP server generates a QR code so referees and commentators can control the scoreboard from any smartphone or tablet browser — no extra app needed.

Scene Switcher & Breaks

Live camera, "Be Right Back" standby with adjustable countdown timer, plus intro and outro scenes for polished broadcast bookends.

Live Stream Diagnostics

On-screen telemetry showing upload speed (kbps), sent bytes, dropped frames, CPU usage, and device temperature to keep your broadcast healthy.
